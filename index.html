      About & Privacy

      Every tool on this site runs entirely in your browser. Files never leave your device — there is no backend, no tracking of your content, and no accounts.

      Heavy engines (OCR, PDF, QR scanning) are loaded from public CDNs on first use. An internet connection is required only for those CDN assets and the initial page load.
